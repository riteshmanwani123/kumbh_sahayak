# Accommodation — general guidance

As of: 2026-10-10
Rule for the assistant: do NOT name or recommend specific hotels, tent operators or prices as official. No official accommodation booking portal has been verified yet. [To be announced]

## Types of stay
- Dharamshalas and ashrams: run by trusts and samaj (community) organisations in Panchavati, Nashik and Trimbak. These are budget options. Reported range is about Rs 200–500 a night. [UNVERIFIED — reported by a travel site] Source: https://urbanchats.com/ultimate-guide-to-kumbh-mela-nashik/
- Hotels and lodges: in Nashik city (College Road, Gangapur Road, Mumbai Naka, Nashik Road) and in Trimbak. Prices go up sharply around snan dates.
- Tent cities: private operators advertise tents (luxury, deluxe, family, dormitory). These are private businesses, not government ones. Check reviews, cancellation terms and the exact location before paying. Example of a private operator site: https://www.thekumbhmelaindia.com/kumbh-mela-dates/ (not endorsed).
- Akhada camps at Sadhugram are for sadhus and their followers, not general tourist lodging.
- Homestays: available, but verify carefully.

## Booking safety
- Book early. Kumbh peak dates (late July to mid-September 2027) fill up fast.
- Beware of fake booking websites and "official tent" scams. Pay only through verified platforms and never pay into personal UPI or bank accounts given by strangers. Report fraud: 1930 (cyber crime) or cybercrime.gov.in.
- If the government launches an official accommodation or e-pass portal, it will be announced on the NTKMA portal: https://divcomnashik.maharashtra.gov.in/en/simhastha-kumbh-mela-2027/

## Where to stay for convenience
- To be near Ramkund, look in the Panchavati area. Expect walking only near the ghats on snan days.
- For Trimbakeshwar: stay in Trimbak town, or in Nashik and travel about 31 km by road or bus.
- If you arrive by train, Nashik Road area stays are near the station, about 12 km from Ramkund.
