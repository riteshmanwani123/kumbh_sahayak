# Dates calendar — Simhastha Kumbh 2026–28 (Nashik & Trimbakeshwar)

As of: 2026-10-10
Primary source (official schedule announced by Maharashtra government with seers and CM Devendra Fadnavis, reported by PTI on 1 June 2025): https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/06/01/bom14-mh-ld-kumbh-mela.html
Confirmed again in news on 20 Sep 2026: https://www.freepressjournal.in/pune/nashik-president-likely-to-attend-simhastha-kumbh-flag-hoisting-ceremony-on-october-31

## Main dates (verified)
| Event | Date | Place |
|---|---|---|
| Flag hoisting (Dhwajarohan), start of Simhastha | Saturday 31 October 2026, 12:02 pm at Ramkund | Ramkund (Panchavati, Nashik) and Kushavarta Tirtha (Trimbakeshwar) |
| Nagar Pradakshina | 29 July 2027 | Nashik |
| First Amrut Snan (Pratham Amrit Snan) | Monday 2 August 2027 (Ashadh Somvati Amavasya) | Nashik and Trimbakeshwar |
| Second Amrut Snan | Tuesday 31 August 2027 (Shravan Amavasya) | Nashik and Trimbakeshwar |
| Third Amrut Snan, Nashik | Saturday 11 September 2027 (Bhadrapada Shuddha Ekadashi) | Ramkund, Nashik |
| Third Amrut Snan, Trimbakeshwar | Sunday 12 September 2027 | Kushavarta, Trimbakeshwar |
| Flag lowering, end of Simhastha | 24 July 2028 (1:36 pm, reported) | Nashik and Trimbakeshwar |

Notes:
- The 12:02 pm flag-hoisting time is from news reports (Free Press Journal, 20 Sep 2026; Urban Chats). Source: https://www.freepressjournal.in/pune/nashik-president-likely-to-attend-simhastha-kumbh-flag-hoisting-ceremony-on-october-31
- The 1:36 pm flag-lowering time is from a travel site. [UNVERIFIED] Source: https://urbanchats.com/ultimate-guide-to-kumbh-mela-nashik/
- "Amrut Snan" and "Amrit Snan" mean the same thing. It used to be called "Shahi Snan" (royal bath).
- Tithi names for the Amrut Snan days come from news reports of the official schedule. Source: https://www.sakshipost.com/news/maha-govt-releases-schedule-2027-nashik-kumbh-mela-preparations-begin-414383

## Other reported dates [UNVERIFIED — travel sites, not the official schedule]
- Akhada flag hoisting at Sadhugram: 24 July 2027. Source: https://urbanchats.com/ultimate-guide-to-kumbh-mela-nashik/
- Peak period: roughly 14 July to 25 September 2027. Source: https://urbanchats.com/ultimate-guide-to-kumbh-mela-nashik/
- Rishi Panchami: 5 Sep 2027; Bhadrapada Purnima: 15 Sep 2027; Maha Shivratri: 27 Feb 2028. Source: https://nashikcity.in/events/nashik-kumbh-mela-2027/

## Old or wrong dates — do NOT use
- Some websites (dated January 2025) list flag hoisting on 14 July 2027 and snans on 26 Aug, 13 Sep, 18 Sep and 25 Sep 2027. These do NOT match the official schedule announced in June 2025. Sources with outdated dates: https://trimbakeshwartemplepujari.com/nashik-kumbh-mela-2027/ and https://trimbakeshwardoshnivarankendra.com/nashik-kumbh-mela-2027/
- One site lists the second Amrut Snan as 11 August 2027. Official reports say 31 August 2027.

## Parva snan (other auspicious bathing days)
- There are 44 bathing occasions in Nashik and 53 in Trimbakeshwar during the Simhastha period. Source: https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/06/01/bom14-mh-ld-kumbh-mela.html
- A full official date list was not found online. [To be announced — check the NTKMA portal https://divcomnashik.maharashtra.gov.in/en/simhastha-kumbh-mela-2027/]

## Time of bathing on Amrut Snan days
- Akhadas bathe first in a fixed order. General pilgrims are usually allowed after the akhada processions finish. Exact timings have not been published. [To be announced]
