# Getting to Nashik and Trimbakeshwar — rail, bus, air, road

As of: 2026-10-10

## By train
- The main railhead is Nashik Road station (code NK), about 12 km by road from Ramkund (OSRM, computed 2026-10-10). Nashik Road is on the Mumbai–Bhusawal main line of Central Railway. Many trains from Mumbai, North India, Central India and East India stop here.
- Railways is preparing five stations for the Simhastha: Nashik Road, Devlali, Odha, Kherwadi and Kasbe Sukene. Works are estimated at over Rs 1,000 crore. Source (AIR News, 25 Jul 2025): https://www.newsonair.gov.in/railways-begin-preparations-for-nashik-trimbakeshwar-simhastha-2027/
- News reports mention about 150 special trains and Igatpuri and Manmad Junction as additional nodes. [UNVERIFIED — exact list of special trains not yet announced] Source: https://navbharatlive.com/maharashtra/nashik/kumbh-mela-nashik-railway-megaplan-150-special-trains-five-stations-1826699.html
- For train timings and PNR status, call Indian Railways enquiry 139 or use the IRCTC / NTES app. Kumbh special train lists: to be announced by Central Railway.
- Mumbai CSMT to Nashik Road takes about 3+ hours by train. Source: https://urbanchats.com/ultimate-guide-to-kumbh-mela-nashik/

## By bus (MSRTC / ST)
- MSRTC (Maharashtra State Road Transport Corporation, the "ST" or "Lal Pari") runs buses to Nashik from across Maharashtra and neighbouring states. Helpline: 1800-22-1250 (24x7). Online booking: https://public.msrtcors.com. [Helpline number from a third-party directory — UNVERIFIED]
- Main bus stands in Nashik: Mahamarg Bus Stand, Thakkar Bazar Bus Stand, CBS (Central Bus Stand), and Nimani (Panchavati, closest to Ramkund).
- During the Kumbh, MSRTC shuttle buses will carry pilgrims from outer parking lots to points near the ghats. About 3,000 MSRTC shuttles are planned on peak days. Source: https://www.punekarnews.in/no-private-vehicle-gets-past-the-outer-ring-on-amrit-snan-days-three-tier-parking-plan-for-nashik-kumbh-2027-takes-shape/
- Mumbai to Nashik by bus takes about 3.5–4.5 hours. Fares reported at around Rs 200–400. [UNVERIFIED — fares change] Source: https://urbanchats.com/ultimate-guide-to-kumbh-mela-nashik/

## By air
- Nashik International Airport is at Ozar (IATA ISK), about 20 km northeast of the city and about 20 km by road from Ramkund. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashik_Airport
- Wikipedia lists IndiGo flights to Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Delhi, Goa and Hyderabad. Routes change often, so check the airline. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashik_Airport
- Alternative: fly to Mumbai (about 180 km away), then take a train or bus. A taxi from Mumbai airport costs roughly Rs 2,000–3,000. [UNVERIFIED] Source: https://urbanchats.com/ultimate-guide-to-kumbh-mela-nashik/
- Shirdi airport is about 96 km by road from Nashik. Flight availability varies.

## By road
- From Mumbai: NH-160 (old NH-3, Mumbai–Agra road) via Thane, Kasara ghat and Igatpuri. You can also use the Samruddhi Mahamarg (Mumbai–Nagpur expressway), which has an Igatpuri/Nashik interchange.
- From Pune: via Sangamner and Sinnar (NH-60), or via the Samruddhi Mahamarg.
- From Gujarat (Surat/Ahmedabad): via Peth / Saputara or via Dhule.
- From North India (Delhi, Indore, Agra): via the Mumbai–Agra road (NH-52/old NH-3) through Dhule and Malegaon.
- Kumbh parking for each entry road: see 05_parking_traffic.md.

## Nashik ↔ Trimbakeshwar
- About 31 km by road (Ramkund to Trimbakeshwar temple), roughly 30–45 minutes on normal days (OSRM, 2026-10-10). Trimbakeshwar is about 26–30 km from Nashik city centre.
- MSRTC runs regular buses from Nashik (CBS / Mahamarg) to Trimbakeshwar. Kumbh shuttle arrangements between the two places are to be announced.
