## Three-tier parking plan (NTKMA)
Source (Punekar News, 10 Jun 2026): https://www.punekarnews.in/no-private-vehicle-gets-past-the-outer-ring-on-amrit-snan-days-three-tier-parking-plan-for-nashik-kumbh-2027-takes-shape/
- Outer 1: backup or contingency parking.
- Outer: catches vehicles at the city entry points.
- Inner: reachable only by MSRTC shuttle buses. This is the last transfer point before the pedestrian-only ghat zone.
- On Amrut Snan days no private vehicle goes past the outer ring. Only Outer and Outer 1 parking operate, and the inner city is vehicle-free.
- Nashik: 46 parking locations over about 888 hectares. Trimbakeshwar: 26 locations over about 671 hectares. Total capacity is about 3 lakh car-equivalent spaces.
- Separate areas for two-wheelers, cars and buses, with basic amenities at each lot.
- About 3,000 MSRTC shuttle buses on peak days.

## Entry-route examples (reported, not final) [UNVERIFIED]
Source: https://nashikkumbhmela.org/simhastha-kumbh-mela-2027-traffic-plan-entry-routes-parking-and-shuttle-bus-system-announced/ (non-government site)
- From Mumbai (NH-3 / Mumbai–Agra road): park near Rajur Bahula, then take the shuttle about 10.3 km to Mahamarg Bus Stand.
- From Pune via Samruddhi Mahamarg: park at Mohagaon / Chincholi, then take the shuttle about 14.4 km to Sinnar Phata Market Yard.
- Private vehicles are expected to be stopped 2–5 km from Ramkund on Amrut Snan days.

## Outer ring road
- A 66-km outer ring road is reported to connect the outer parking lots. [UNVERIFIED — completion status unknown]

## What to tell callers
- On snan days, plan to park outside the city and use the shuttle buses. Do not try to drive to Ramkund or Kushavarta.
- Keep your parking lot name or number and the shuttle route written down or photographed.
- Exact lot names, diversions and shuttle timings will be announced by Nashik City Police (traffic branch) and NTKMA. Until then, say "to be announced".
