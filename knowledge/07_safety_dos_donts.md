# Safety, do's and don'ts, lost & found, accessibility, weather

As of: 2026-10-10
Note: this is general safety guidance written for this assistant, based on standard crowd-safety practice and the Nashik DDMA monsoon advisory. It is not an official Kumbh rule list. Official Kumbh rules will be published by NTKMA and Nashik Police. [To be announced]

## Crowd safety (general guidance)
- Follow police and volunteer instructions, barricades and one-way routes. Do not cross barricades.
- Do not run, push or stop suddenly in a crowd. If the crowd surges, move sideways (diagonally) out of the flow, and keep your arms up in front of your chest to protect your breathing.
- If you fall, get up quickly. If you cannot get up, curl up on your side and protect your head.
- After bathing, leave the ghat quickly so others can come in. Do not sit or stand on the ghat steps.
- Avoid peak hours on Amrut Snan days if you are elderly, unwell or travelling with small children. Bathing on other parva days is also auspicious.
- In 2003 a stampede near Ramkund killed 39 people, so crowd rules matter. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashik-Trimbakeshwar_Simhastha

## Do's
- Carry ID (Aadhaar or other) and a paper with your name, your companion's phone number and where you are staying. Put the same note in children's and elders' pockets.
- Pick a fixed meeting point (for example a named gate or police booth) before entering the crowd.
- Keep your phone charged and carry a small power bank. Note 112 as the emergency number.
- Drink water from safe sources, and carry ORS in hot or humid weather.
- Use the public toilets provided. Keep the Godavari clean.
- Keep valuables to a minimum and use a front pouch.

## Don'ts
- Do not enter deep water or swim. Bathe only in the marked bathing area, especially during monsoon when the river can rise fast.
- Do not use soap, shampoo or detergent in the river, and do not throw plastic, flowers in plastic bags, or waste into it.
- Do not believe rumours. Check with police or official announcements.
- Do not hand over money or documents to people claiming to offer "VIP darshan" or "special passes". Report fraud to 112 or the cyber helpline 1930.
- Do not fly drones without permission.

## Lost and found (khoya-paya)
1. Stay calm and stay where you are, if safe. Go to the nearest police officer, volunteer or police booth.
2. Call 112. For a lost child, also call Childline 1098.
3. Give the person's name, age, clothing colour, language, photo (if you have one on your phone) and the last place you saw them.
4. Go to the nearest Khoya-Paya (lost and found) centre. 2027 centre locations: to be announced.
5. The assistant can log a demo ticket with create_ticket and give a ticket ID. This is not an official police record.

## Accessibility
- Elderly people, people with disabilities and pregnant women should avoid peak Amrut Snan hours if possible.
- Whether special access ghats, wheelchairs or e-carts will be provided for 2027 has not been announced. [To be announced]
- On snan days the last 2–5 km to Ramkund is expected to be on foot, after the shuttle bus drop. Plan for that walk. Source: https://nashikkumbhmela.org/simhastha-kumbh-mela-2027-traffic-plan-entry-routes-parking-and-shuttle-bus-system-announced/ [UNVERIFIED]

## Weather and season
- The three Amrut Snans (2 Aug, 31 Aug, 11/12 Sep 2027) fall in the south-west monsoon season. Expect rain, slippery ghats and possible rises in the Godavari water level. Carry a raincoat or umbrella and footwear with good grip.
- The Nashik District Disaster Management Authority (DDMA) urges citizens to stay alert in the monsoon. Disaster helpline: 1077, Police: 112, Ambulance: 108, Fire: 101. Source: https://www.freepressjournal.in/pune/nashik-ddma-urges-citizens-to-stay-alert-and-follow-safety-measures-during-monsoon
- The flag hoisting (31 Oct 2026) is in the post-monsoon season: usually warm days and cooler nights.
- Nashik city is at about 560–600 m altitude, so it is a little milder than Mumbai. Trimbakeshwar and Brahmagiri are hilly and get heavy rain in the monsoon.
