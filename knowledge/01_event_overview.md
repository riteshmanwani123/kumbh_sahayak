# Event overview — Nashik–Trimbakeshwar Simhastha Kumbh Mela 2026–28

As of: 2026-10-10

## What it is
- The Simhastha Kumbh Mela is a Hindu religious fair held about every 12 years in Nashik district, Maharashtra, on the banks of the Godavari river. It is held when Jupiter (Guru) is in Leo (Simha), which is why it is called "Simhastha". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashik-Trimbakeshwar_Simhastha
- It takes place at two places at the same time: Nashik city (Ramkund, Panchavati) and Trimbakeshwar (Kushavarta Kund), about 30 km apart by road. Source: https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/06/01/bom14-mh-ld-kumbh-mela.html
- The Simhastha period formally starts with flag hoisting (Dhwajarohan) on 31 October 2026 and ends with flag lowering on 24 July 2028. The biggest crowds come on the three Amrut Snan (royal bath) days in August–September 2027. Source: https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/06/01/bom14-mh-ld-kumbh-mela.html

## Vaishnav and Shaiva akhadas — who bathes where
- Historically the fair was held only at Trimbak. After a violent clash between Shaiva and Vaishnava ascetics in 1789, the Peshwa moved the Vaishnava akhadas to Ramkund in Nashik. Since then Vaishnava akhadas bathe at Ramkund (Nashik) and Shaiva akhadas bathe at Kushavarta (Trimbakeshwar). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashik-Trimbakeshwar_Simhastha
- This is why the third Amrut Snan is on 11 Sep 2027 in Nashik and 12 Sep 2027 in Trimbakeshwar.
- 13 akhadas are reported to take part: Juna, Niranjani, Mahanirvani, Atal, Ahwan, Nirmohi, Anand, Panchagni, Nagpanthi Gorakhnath, Vaishnav, Udasin Panchayati Bada, Udasin Naya, Nirmal Panchayati. ## Scale
- About 3 crore (30 million) devotees came in 2015. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashik-Trimbakeshwar_Simhastha
- Estimates for 2027 vary a lot between sources (1.2 crore to 4 crore+). [UNVERIFIED]
- The state government reported tenders of about Rs 4,000 crore issued, about Rs 2,000 crore more coming, and Rs 681 crore for Choundi heritage development (as of June 2025). Source: https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/06/01/bom14-mh-ld-kumbh-mela.html
- Nashik has 44 auspicious bathing occasions and Trimbakeshwar 53 (including Ekadashi, Purnima and Amavasya days) during the Simhastha period. Source: https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/06/01/bom14-mh-ld-kumbh-mela.html

## Safety history (why crowd rules matter)
- In 2003 a stampede near Ramkund killed 39 pilgrims. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashik-Trimbakeshwar_Simhastha
- Lesson for pilgrims: follow police barricades and one-way routes, do not rush, and do not stop on ghats after bathing.
