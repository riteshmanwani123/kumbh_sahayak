## Nashik city (Vaishnava akhadas bathe here)

### Ramkund (रामकुंड) — main bathing ghat in Nashik
- Location: Panchavati, Nashik, on the Godavari. Approx 20.00726, 73.79249.
- Main Amrut Snan site in Nashik and the venue for the 31 Oct 2026 flag hoisting at 12:02 pm. Source: https://www.freepressjournal.in/pune/nashik-president-likely-to-attend-simhastha-kumbh-flag-hoisting-ceremony-on-october-31
- About 12 km by road from Nashik Road railway station and about 20 km from Ozar airport (OSRM road distance, computed 2026-10-10).
- On Amrut Snan days private vehicles are stopped 2–5 km away. The last stretch is on foot. Source: https://www.punekarnews.in/no-private-vehicle-gets-past-the-outer-ring-on-amrit-snan-days-three-tier-parking-plan-for-nashik-kumbh-2027-takes-shape/

### Panchavati (पंचवटी)
- The old temple area on the left bank of the Godavari around Ramkund. Approx 20.00668, 73.79357.
- Includes Kalaram Mandir (approx 20.00695, 73.79522, about 300 m east of Ramkund) and Sita Gufa (approx 20.00772, 73.79616).

### Tapovan (तपोवन) and Sadhugram (साधुग्राम)
- Tapovan is approx 20.00078, 73.81258, about 2.5 km east of Ramkund along the river. Kapila–Godavari Sangam is nearby (approx 19.99968, 73.81549).
- Sadhugram is the camp area for sadhus and akhadas on the Tapovan side. Exact boundaries for 2027 are not published. [UNVERIFIED]
- Walking from Ramkund to Tapovan is about 2.8 km, roughly 35–40 minutes (OSRM foot routing, computed 2026-10-10).

### Other places in Nashik
- Godavari ghats near Ramkund: several ghats line both banks near Ramkund (Gandhi Talav and others). Names of the official bathing ghats for 2027 have not been published. [To be announced]
- Someshwar (Gangapur Road), approx 20.02920, 73.72340: a waterfall and temple area upstream.
- Muktidham Temple, Nashik Road: about 6 km from central Nashik (travel site). [UNVERIFIED] Source: https://urbanchats.com/ultimate-guide-to-kumbh-mela-nashik/

## Trimbakeshwar (Shaiva akhadas bathe here)

### Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga Temple (त्र्यंबकेश्वर)
- One of the 12 Jyotirlingas, in Trimbak town. Approx 19.93212, 73.53075.
- About 31 km by road from Ramkund, roughly 30–45 minutes on normal days (OSRM, computed 2026-10-10). Expect much longer on snan days.

### Kushavarta Kund / Kushavarta Tirtha (कुशावर्त कुंड)
- The sacred tank in Trimbak where Shaiva akhadas take the Amrut Snan. It is considered the symbolic source of the Godavari. Approx 19.93274, 73.52761 (OSM "Kushawart Tirth"), a short walk from Trimbakeshwar temple.
- The Trimbakeshwar flag hoisting on 31 Oct 2026 is held here. Source: https://www.freepressjournal.in/pune/nashik-president-likely-to-attend-simhastha-kumbh-flag-hoisting-ceremony-on-october-31

### Around Trimbak
- Brahmagiri hill (origin of the Godavari): approx 19.91519, 73.52360.
- Anjaneri (believed to be Hanuman's birthplace): approx 19.94602, 73.58685, on the Nashik–Trimbak road.
