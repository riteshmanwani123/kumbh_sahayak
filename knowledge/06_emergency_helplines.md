## Lost and found (khoya-paya)
- If a person is lost: call 112 immediately, tell the nearest police officer or volunteer, and go to the nearest Khoya-Paya (lost and found) centre or police booth. For a lost child, also call Childline 1098.
- Kumbh lost-and-found centre locations for 2027: to be announced. [To be announced]
- The assistant can register a lost-person ticket with create_ticket. This is a demo log, not an official police complaint.
